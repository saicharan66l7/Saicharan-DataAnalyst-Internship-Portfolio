I brought the complete internship journey together into a master GitHub portfolio containing my projects, learnings, presentation and portfolio website.

### What I Learned Most

One of my biggest learnings from this internship was that a good analysis needs more than numbers and charts.

The complete process is:

Clean the data → Explore the data → Find patterns → Validate the findings → Explain the business impact

This helped me understand how a data analyst can move from raw data to a useful business decision.


### Biggest Statistical Learning

Task 4 helped me understand an important concept in statistics.

A visible difference between two groups does not automatically mean that the difference is statistically significant.

By using hypothesis testing and p-values, I learned how to evaluate whether the available data provides enough evidence to support a difference or association.

The four tests in Task 4 did not provide statistically significant evidence at the 0.05 significance level for the business questions tested.
