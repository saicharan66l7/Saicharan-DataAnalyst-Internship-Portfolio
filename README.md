## 🔑 Key Findings Across All Tasks

### From Data Cleaning (Task 1)
- 20 missing Age values imputed, 9 duplicate Order IDs flagged
- 33 data quality issues resolved, 15 new features engineered
- Dataset grew from 12 → 27 columns — analysis-ready

### From EDA (Task 2)
- **Electronics = 35.4%** of all orders — top revenue category
- **Unit Price (r=0.69)** is the strongest revenue driver
- Revenue is consistent year-round — no major seasonality
- Near **50-50 gender split** across all categories

### From Statistical Validation (Task 4)
- All 4 hypotheses **failed to reject H0** at alpha = 0.05
- No significant spending difference by gender or day of week
- Simplified marketing strategy is statistically justified
